Episode I: The Cyber Menace

In an age where information reigns supreme, the peaceful Digital Republic is under threat from the sinister forces of the Dark Hackers. These digital Sith have discovered a critical vulnerability in the Republic's defenses, threatening the safety and security of systems galaxy-wide.

Amidst the turmoil, a group of brave Code Jedis emerges, wielding the power of knowledge and cybersecurity skills. They are the galaxy's last hope to defend against the growing darkness of cyber threats and to restore balance to the network.

Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to join the ranks of these Code Jedis. Learn the ways of cybersecurity, master the art of digital ethical hacking, and use your skills to explore, defend, and conquer the challenges that lie ahead.

The path is fraught with peril, with traps set by the Sith Hackers, luring unwary travelers with deceitful memes and cunning digital riddles. Only the most skilled and wise will navigate these challenges, unmask the vulnerabilities, and strike back to protect the Digital Republic.

Instructions

Your main goal is to hack into Emperor Palpatine's Dark Archive. This daunting task can only be achieved by retrieving the login credentials and the password. Our informants say the Emperor is famously lazy about picking passwords - once you have the username, a little patience should get you the rest. To accomplish this, you must navigate through a series of intricate cyber challenges below, each testing your skills in a different area of hacking. From all these challenges, you will be able to piece together the secret password and login details. Find every flag you can as you fight your way in. Remember, the path is laden with traps and deceptions set by the Sith. Stay vigilant, employ your wits, and may the force of cybersecurity be with you.

Loose Lips

Web Security Level 1 150 pts
Your task will be to get to know the Imperial Command. And what's a better way to DO IT than visiting their own page. The page is called ImperialNet. The Rebel Alliance created this tool ImperialWebscraper especially for your mission, it could help you collect the data from ImperialNet. Our intelligence told us that on this page the highest in command are writing about themselves in a self-aggrandizing manner. Study these profiles carefully - not everything worth finding is listed on the front page.

Whispers in the Wire

Web Security Level 2 250 pts
The Dark Hackers often communicate through coded messages hidden in plain sight. Somewhere on ImperialNet, visitors are free to leave their thoughts for everyone to read - and nobody has ever checked too closely what those thoughts are actually made of. See what you can get away with.

Above Your Clearance

Web Security Level 2 250 pts
The Empire, in its arrogance, has overlooked something in how it decides who's allowed to see what. Some doors that should check your credentials first... don't, not carefully enough. Tread carefully, as the line between forbidden knowledge and a trap can be thin.

The Sith Database

Web Security Level 3 350 pts
Deep within the dark-holocron, a login page built by Darth Vader trusts your input a little too much. If you can make the query say something it wasn't meant to say, you might convince it to let you in further than a Sith Lord ever intended - and further still, if you can be precise about exactly who you're asking for. Remember, the Sith are cunning - expect traps at each turn.

Beyond the Throne Room

Bonus Level 4 450 pts
Rumor in the barracks says the Emperor's own terminal holds more than ceremony - and that not even an Emperor gets to see everything on his own machine. If you've truly earned his login, it might be worth finding out where else it still works. Entirely optional, but the truly dedicated may find one more secret buried past the throne room.
